Connect JupyterLab to Claude

Claude works in your JupyterLab (for example on the HPC, through your SSH tunnel) through the browser tab you are logged in to. It never needs your Jupyter password.

Claude 通过你已登录的 JupyterLab 标签页工作，不需要你的 Jupyter 密码。

  1. Once: drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar (show the bar with ⌘⇧B in Chrome).
    Claude ⇄ Jupyter
    只需一次：把上面的按钮拖到书签栏。
  2. Each time: open JupyterLab and log in as usual, then click the bookmark in that tab. A small Claude ⇄ Jupyter: Claude connected badge appears in its bottom right corner.
    每次：打开 JupyterLab、照常输入密码登录，然后在那个标签页里点一下这个书签。
  3. Ask Claude to connect to JupyterLab. Follow the notebook on the Jupyter mirror.

Good to know

The bookmark holds no secret: it loads the bridge script from Claude's hub on this computer, which then talks to the Jupyter server only through that tab. Reloading or closing the tab disconnects it; click the bookmark again after a reload. If you click it in a second JupyterLab tab, that tab takes over.
